TECHMART SHIPPING POLICY

1. Standard shipping takes 3–5 business days.

2. Express shipping takes 1–2 business days.

3. Standard shipping is free for orders above ₹1,000.

4. Orders below ₹1,000 have a ₹99 standard shipping fee.

5. Express shipping costs ₹199.

6. Customers receive a tracking number after the order is shipped.

7. Delivery times may be longer during holidays or unexpected disruptions.

8. TechMart currently delivers within India.
