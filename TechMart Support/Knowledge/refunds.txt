TECHMART REFUND POLICY

1. Refunds are processed after the returned product passes inspection.

2. Approved refunds are normally processed within 5–7 business days.

3. Refunds are issued to the original payment method.

4. Shipping charges may not be refundable unless the return is due to a TechMart error.

5. If a refund has not been received after the expected period, the customer should contact TechMart support.
