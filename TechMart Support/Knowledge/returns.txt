TECHMART RETURN POLICY

1. Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery.

2. The product must be unused and in its original condition.

3. The original packaging and included accessories should be available.

4. Products damaged due to customer misuse may not be eligible for return.

5. Some products may have additional return restrictions.

6. Customers should contact TechMart support to initiate a return.

7. A returned product may be inspected before the return is approved.
