TECHMART FAQ

Q: What is TechMart?
A: TechMart is an online electronics store serving customers in India.

Q: How can I track my order?
A: Customers receive a tracking number after their order is shipped.

Q: How long does standard delivery take?
A: Standard delivery normally takes 3–5 business days.

Q: How long does express delivery take?
A: Express delivery normally takes 1–2 business days.

Q: How long do I have to return a product?
A: Customers can request a return within 7 days of delivery.

Q: How long do refunds take?
A: Approved refunds are normally processed within 5–7 business days after inspection.

Q: Where does TechMart deliver?
A: TechMart currently delivers within India.
